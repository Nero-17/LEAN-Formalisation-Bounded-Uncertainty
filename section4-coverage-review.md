## Independently verified source issue and non-issues

**S4-13b: a genuine scope failure for arbitrary subsets, not a refutation of the compact-set theory.** Work in the plane with X the whole space, f the identity, and constant radius one. These satisfy all system and dual positive-radius/contraction assumptions. Take A to be the open unit ball and y=(2,0). Its distance to A is one. Nevertheless, for every x in A, the reverse triangle inequality gives dist(y,x) at least 2−norm(x), which is strictly greater than one. Thus y belongs to the weak distance sublevel set and to no dual ball centred at a point of A. The explicit universal wording in 4.13/4.15 admits this A; the later results under the nonempty compact A convention do not. A formal certificate and the corrected closed-set distance theorem remain to be connected in Lean. No paper edit is authorised by this report.
